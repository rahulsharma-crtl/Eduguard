EDUGUARD · OPTIONAL RESEARCH PAGE

Facial-behavior validation

One supervised session · approximately 75–100 seconds of guided capture · five actions. This measures camera-based behavior detection, not attention, learning or misconduct.

Your camera stays on this device. No audio is requested. No video, images, landmarks or results are uploaded. Browser code/model assets are downloaded. Only derived labels and study metadata are saved locally in this tab for recovery and exported as one CSV. Random IDs are pseudonymous; timestamps and conditions can still reveal information.

Read the consent information with your supervising observer.

Before starting

Participation is voluntary and has no effect on grades. Stop at any time. Hold each pose comfortably; do not strain or continue if uncomfortable. Your observer will read the instructions while your eyes are closed. The study supervisor must explain who will receive your CSV, how long it will be retained, and how to request withdrawal.

No teacher account, invitation, classroom or backend connection is needed. Face the camera in even lighting. This protocol uses the engine defaults without personal threshold or camera-offset calibration.

English narration uses a local browser voice when available; otherwise the observer reads the visible instructions.

Camera readiness

  • Camera permission: waiting
  • Face visibility estimate: waiting
  • Tracking quality: waiting

This check is advisory, not a reference label. Unstable tracking may remain unknown. Do not exclude a participant just because the model performs poorly.

Prepare to begin

Predictions are hidden. Observation starts only after narration ends and a five-second settling interval.

Observer: click once when you independently see the requested action begin. Never infer verification from the mesh or software quality indicators.

Independent observer verification

The camera is stopped. Confirm each action based on what you personally observed, not the software. Invalid means the instructed action was not performed as specified; uncertain means you cannot establish the reference. Predictions remain hidden until these decisions are locked.

Your CSV is ready

The one session CSV contains trial summaries, eight sampling windows per action, and underlying repeated observations. It contains no camera images or landmarks. Re-downloading produces the same session file, not a new trial.

This clears only the isolated study record. It does not delete an exported file or any classroom data. Do not repeat a participant’s experiment to select a better result.
